# Example grammar fix report

- **Total checked:** 502
- **Fixed:** 40
- **Left alone:** 462
- **exampleZh updated:** 40 (zh-HK Traditional Chinese, manual)

## What was fixed

Issues found across all 502 `example` sentences: missing articles on countable nouns, noun/verb part-of-speech mismatches, missing prepositions, incomplete sentences, awkward phrasing, and US spelling where the project uses British/HK English.

## Before → after (all fixes)

### `animal` (animal)
- **Before:** I like the animal at the zoo.
- **After:** I saw an animal at the zoo.
- **exampleZh:** 我喺動物園見到一隻動物。
- **Why:** awkward definite singular; saw + an is natural

### `biscuit` (biscuit)
